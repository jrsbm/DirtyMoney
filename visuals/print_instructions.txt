Folder visuals/boards contains the two boards for the game.
- One for playing with 5 or 6 players
- One for playing with 7 or 8 players

Folder visuals/cards contains the cards for the game.
card_print.txt lists the number of cards you must print for a complete set.

Other materials
- dirty_money_rules.docx contains a printable instruction to the game.
- 8 envelopes are necessary to secretly assign players their role.
- 8 coins or similar are necessary for the game's economy element.
